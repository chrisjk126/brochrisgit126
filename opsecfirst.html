Official OPay Security First Guidelines

Proactive protection measures, fraud detection standards, credential safety, and emergency response procedures.

Emergency Account Lock: Dial *955#

If your phone is lost, stolen, or you suspect unauthorized access, dial *955# immediately from any mobile line to instantly freeze your OPay account and protect your funds.

1. Credential Security & Anti-Phishing

Rules and habits to ensure your login details and transaction PINs remain safe.

The "Zero Share" Rule

  • Never disclose your 4-digit PIN, OTPs, or passwords to anyone.
  • OPay staff will never ask for your security credentials via phone or email.
  • Ignore unverified social media handles claiming to offer fast customer resolution.

Device Binding & Biometrics

  • Accounts are cryptographically linked to your registered primary smartphone.
  • New device logins require 2FA multi-factor checks and biometric verification.
  • Enable fingerprint or Face ID for fast and secure transaction authorizations.

Phishing Awareness

  • Always confirm website links end with official domains before entering data.
  • Do not click on unverified SMS links promising free cash or promo awards.
  • Report suspicious calls or phishing attempts directly via in-app support channels.

2. Fraud Monitoring & Emergency Controls

Behind-the-scenes automated monitoring systems and quick action tools.

Automated Risk Engine

  • 24/7 AI monitoring flags rapid high-volume transfers to unfamiliar accounts.
  • Geographic anomaly detection prevents unauthorized remote card usage.
  • High-risk transfers trigger mandatory live selfie liveness checks.

Custom Transfer Limits

  • Set personalized daily transfer limits to minimize potential loss risks.
  • Activate "Nighttime Security Lock" to restrict transfers during late hours.
  • Customize approval rules for transactions exceeding set financial thresholds.

Incident Reporting & Claims

  • Log fraudulent debits immediately via the Report Issue tab.
  • Instant card blocking available directly inside the OPay app card section.
  • 24/7 dedicated anti-fraud helpline available to trace and reverse flagged funds.
