Official OPay Privacy Policy

Principles governing data collection, privacy compliance, user consent, third-party sharing, and personal data security.

NDPR & NDPA 2023 Compliance Guarantee

OPay strictly adheres to the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) and Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). Your personal and financial data is encrypted and processed in full compliance with central banking regulations and privacy laws.

1. Information Collection & Usage Principles

What data we collect, how we collect it, and the legal basis for processing.

Identity & KYC Information

  • Collection of full name, phone number, email address, and residential address.
  • Mandatory verification of NIN and BVN mandated by CBN AML/CFT regulations.
  • Biometric data collection (live selfies) for facial identification and fraud protection.

Technical & Device Telemetry

  • IP address, device hardware model, OS version, and unique device identifier logs.
  • Real-time geolocation tracking for fraud monitoring and terminal location validation.
  • In-app usage logs and cookie identifiers to enhance account session stability.

Core Processing Purposes

  • Provision and maintenance of wallet accounts, payment routing, and savings.
  • Automated transaction monitoring and anti-fraud fraud screening.
  • Mandatory reporting and regulatory compliance updates to financial authorities.

2. Third-Party Sharing & User Rights

How data is shared with partners, retained, and protected under privacy rights.

Third-Party Disclosures

  • Data shared exclusively with regulatory bodies (CBN, NDIC, NIBSS, NCDC).
  • Integration with accredited identity verification and credit bureau partners.
  • Encrypted data storage on ISO 27001-certified secure cloud infrastructure.

Data Security & Encryption

  • TLS 1.3 protocol encryption for all data in transit across mobile endpoints.
  • AES-256 bit encryption applied to sensitive customer data at rest.
  • Annual independent third-party vulnerability assessments and penetration tests.

Data Subject Rights

  • Right to request access to or copies of your personal stored data.
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate or outdated profile details.
  • Direct escalation line to Data Protection Officer at dpo@opay-inc.com.
