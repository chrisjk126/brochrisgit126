Recruitment Integrity Notice

OPay will never ask candidates for money, processing fees, or registration deposits at any point in the recruitment process. Apply exclusively through official channels.

1. Recruitment Process Guidelines

Our standardized selection pipeline ensures fair and merit-based evaluation.

Online Application & Screening

  • Apply through official job portals and verified platforms.
  • Ensure CV contains measurable achievements and role-relevant skills.
  • Credentials and work background undergo automated screening.

Assessment & Role Simulations

  • Competency assessment tests evaluate problem-solving capabilities.
  • Technical roles complete practical tasks (coding/system design/case studies).
  • Customer-facing roles complete communication & mock scenario tasks.

Interviews & Final Offer

  • Structured interviews with Talent Acquisition and Department Leads.
  • Rigorous background checks and identity verification for finalists.
  • Formal employment offer with transparent onboarding parameters.

2. Work Culture & Early Talent Pathways

How we foster excellence, reward high performers, and develop future leaders.

Performance-Driven Culture

  • Clear Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) set for every role.
  • Fast career progression based on merit, impact, and results.
  • Encourages accountability, innovation, and proactive execution.

OPay Futures & Internships

  • Management Trainee tracks designed for top recent graduates.
  • Structured internship programs for final-year university students.
  • Direct mentorship and cross-functional exposure across fintech operations.

Employee Benefits & Support

  • Competitive compensation packages and performance incentives.
  • Comprehensive Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) coverage.
  • Statutory pension contributions and continuous learning opportunities.
