Official OPay Money Transfer Guidelines

Operational rules, settlement timelines, transaction limits, and resolution policies for instant transfers.

Automated Reversal Guarantee

If your account is debited during bank network downtime but the recipient does not receive the funds, your wallet will automatically be refunded within 24 hours. Report unresolved debits directly in your transaction history tab.

1. KYC Transfer Tiers & Limits

Daily and single transfer ceilings dictated by central banking regulations.

Tier 1 Account Limits

  • Maximum Daily Transfer: ₦50,000.
  • Maximum Balance Capacity: ₦300,000.
  • Requires basic phone number registration and NIN link.

Tier 2 Account Limits

  • Maximum Daily Transfer: ₦200,000.
  • Maximum Balance Capacity: ₦500,000.
  • Requires verified BVN and valid government-issued ID card.

Tier 3 & Merchant Limits

  • Maximum Daily Transfer: ₦5,000,000+.
  • Maximum Balance Capacity: Unlimited.
  • Requires residential address verification and full KYC documentation.

2. Verification & Dispute Resolution Standards

Safety protocols to ensure accurate delivery and dispute tracking.

Name Match Verification

  • Always confirm the retrieved account name before authorizing transfers.
  • Transfers sent to wrong account numbers cannot be recalled automatically.
  • Cross-check bank name selections to prevent routing errors.

Biometric Authorizations

  • Transfers exceeding custom limits mandate facial recognition verification.
  • PIN prompts timeout after 60 seconds of inactivity to prevent hijacking.
  • Repeated wrong PIN entries trigger temporary 24-hour transfer locks.

Dispute Logging Protocols

  • Select transaction in the OPay app and click "Report Issue".
  • NIBSS Session IDs are generated instantly to track interbank claims.
  • Disputes are resolved within 1 to 3 business days alongside partner banks.
