1. Interest Rates & Calculation Standards

How daily interest is calculated, tiered, and remitted into your balance.

Tiered Return Rates

  • Earn up to 15% p.a. interest on standard balance thresholds.
  • Tiered calculation ensures optimal returns for micro and medium savings.
  • Rates are reviewed periodically in accordance with central bank yields.

Daily Interest Credit

  • Interest is calculated daily based on midnight account balances.
  • Payouts are credited directly to your OWealth account balance every morning.
  • Compounding effect occurs as daily interest adds to overall capital.

Taxation & Deductions

  • A 10% Withholding Tax (WHT) applies to earned interest as required by law.
  • Tax is automatically deducted prior to daily interest disbursement.
  • No administrative, maintenance, or hidden transaction fees charged.

2. Liquidity Rules & Auto-Save Features

Rules governing instant withdrawals, spending, and automated savings routines.

Zero Lock-In Period

  • Withdraw or spend your OWealth funds at any time without penalties.
  • No lock-in period required to qualify for accrued daily interest earnings.
  • Use OWealth balance directly for debit card payments and transfer transactions.

Auto-Invest Settings

  • Enable "Auto-Save" to sweep fresh OPay wallet balances into OWealth automatically.
  • Set customized daily or weekly deposit schedules straight from your bank card.
  • Pause or disable automated deposits at any time via the app settings.

Safety & Capital Protection

  • Principal deposits are backed by licensed microfinance operations.

  • Biometric confirmation required for large withdrawals or setting updates.
