Official OPay Press Guidelines

Guidelines for media representatives, journalists, and brand partners covering OPay news, announcements, and visual assets.

Official Media Desk & Contact

For urgent press inquiries, official statements, or interview scheduling with OPay executives, contact our Corporate Communications team at press@opay-inc.com or partnerships@opay-inc.com.

1. Media & Spokesperson Protocols

Rules regarding official communications, interviews, and press statements.

Authorized Spokespersons

  • Only designated PR representatives and C-Suite Executives speak on behalf of OPay.
  • Comments from unverified employees do not constitute official company positions.
  • All interview requests must be submitted 48 hours prior to scheduling.

Fact Verification & Reporting

  • Journalists should verify user numbers, metrics, or revenue stats with OPay PR.
  • Off-the-record discussions require explicit mutual agreement before interviews.
  • Embargoed press releases must not be published prior to the specified release time.

Crisis Communications

  • All inquiries regarding security incidents or audits are routed to PR.
  • OPay releases official statements exclusively through verified newsroom portals.
  • Speculative reporting without official confirmation is strongly discouraged.

2. Brand Assets & Corporate Usage

Standards for using OPay logos, photography, and standard description text.

Logo & Media Kit Usage

  • Use high-resolution vectors from the official OPay Media Kit.
  • Do not alter primary logo colors (`#00B875`), proportions, or typography.
  • Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo in all publication layouts.

Approved Corporate Boilerplate

  • Identify OPay as a "leading financial services platform licensed by the CBN".
  • Reference deposit insurance coverage under the NDIC framework in financial press.
  • Use accurate terminology: "OPay Digital Services Limited".

Photography & Multimedia

  • Media may use executive headshots supplied in the press kit.
  • Product UI screenshots must showcase the latest app interface version.
  • Video footage requests require advance written approval from Corporate Communications.
