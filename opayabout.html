1. Account Security & Protection Features

Rules and tools designed to prevent unauthorized account access.

NightGuard Protection

  • Protects transactions between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM automatically.
  • Mandates facial recognition verification for overnight transfers.
  • Guards your money against unauthorized nighttime access.

Large Transaction Shield

  • Set customized spending limits for outgoing transactions.
  • Requires facial recognition for any transfer exceeding your limit.
  • Allows daily and monthly total limit customization.

Safety PIN & Emergency Lock

  • Use a secondary Safety PIN under pressure to silently restrict transfers.
  • Use 1-Tap Emergency Lock to freeze all transfers during suspicions.
  • Prevents instant loss from physical duress or scam attempts.

2. Credential Safety & Verification Rules

Mandatory compliance guidelines for all active account owners.

PIN & OTP Integrity

  • Never disclose your 4-digit PIN, OTP, or CVV to anyone.
  • OPay representatives will never request your PIN or passwords.
  • Change PIN credentials every 60–90 days for safety.

KYC Compliance Tiers

  • Link valid identity credentials (NIN & BVN) to raise limits.
  • Ensure registration phone numbers match official network records.
  • Tier upgrades expand daily transaction and deposit capacity.

Verified Installation Rules

  • Download updates only from Google Play and Apple App Store.
  • Never download `.APK` files shared via WhatsApp, Telegram, or blogs.
  • Verify publisher name is OPay Digital Services.
