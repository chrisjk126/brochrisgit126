Official OPay POS Terminal Guidelines

Merchant operational policies, settlement terms, transaction thresholds, and security compliance for POS operations.

Instant T+0 Wallet Settlement Guarantee

All card payments, transfer-to-POS, and QR code transactions processed on OPay Smart POS terminals settle instantly into your merchant wallet 24/7 without delays or end-of-day batch processing requirements.

1. Terminal Acquisition & Target Thresholds

Leasing rules, cautionary deposits, and activity requirements for agents and merchants.

Cautionary Deposit System

  • Terminals are issued under cautionary deposit models rather than outright sales.
  • Deposit is fully refundable upon terminal return in undamaged working condition.
  • Requires verified business address, CAC documents (for merchants), and NIN/BVN.

Monthly Transaction Targets

  • Merchants must meet daily/monthly minimum transaction volume thresholds.
  • Inactive terminals (zero transactions for 14+ consecutive days) trigger auto-flagging.
  • Unproductive terminals may be retrieved by assigned Relationship Managers.

SIM & Network Operations

  • Terminals come equipped with dual 4G roaming SIMs for maximum uptime.
  • Merchants must not swap, remove, or use embedded SIM cards in personal phones.
  • Wi-Fi connectivity can be configured as a backup for high-volume store locations.

2. Chargebacks, Dispute Resolution & Compliance

Operational standards to prevent fraud, chargeback losses, and policy violations.

Prohibited Practices

  • Exorbitant customer cash advance surcharges beyond approved CBN rates are banned.
  • Sub-leasing or transferring POS terminals to unapproved third parties is prohibited.
  • Splitting single transactions across multiple cards to evade limits is strictly restricted.

Receipt & Print Requirements

  • Always provide customers with the official printed transaction merchant copy.
  • In case of paper roll depletion, digital receipts can be resent via SMS/Email in-app.
  • Declined transactions must be verified via receipt status before retrying payments.

Disputes & Hardware Maintenance

  • Debited-not-credited disputes can be logged immediately on the Merchant App.
  • Failed printer hardware, battery faults, or screen damage must be reported to RMs.
  • Unauthorized repairs by third-party technicians invalidate terminal warranties.
